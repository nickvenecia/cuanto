# Changelog

Plain words for the people using Cuánto. Every version's entries are what its Release and the update window say.

## 1.1.1

- Fixed a crash when saving your profile: Add Profile, Save Changes or Delete Profile… in Settings › Profiles
  could close Cuánto.
- Fixed a crash when right-clicking a budget in the sidebar and choosing Delete… or Duplicate.

## 1.1.0

- Make your own templates: Save as Template… keeps a budget's categories, accounts, lines, globals, fringes and
  fees (and its rates, if you like) to start new budgets from. Its client, dates and spending stay behind.
- New Budget lists your templates under Cuánto's, and offers the one you used last.
- Rename or delete your templates in Settings › Templates; undo works for them too.
- Words are never cut off: on computers that draw letters wider, a start tile's line goes on to a second line.

## 1.0.0

- Build film and video budgets the way the industry reads them: a top sheet of categories (Above the Line,
  Production, Post Production, Other), the accounts under each, and the lines in each account.
- Every line is so many, of so many units (days, weeks, hours, flat, allowance…), at a rate. The total
  works itself out as you type, and so does every account, category, section and the grand total.
- Globals: name a number once (shoot days, prep days, crew size) and use it in any line. Change it and the
  whole budget follows.
- Fringes: payroll taxes, union pension & health, workers' comp. A percent of each line (with an optional
  cap) or a flat amount per unit; switch them on line by line, or for a whole account at once.
- Contingency, a production fee and a markup on the top sheet, each a percent of what you choose or a flat
  amount.
- Start from a template: an indie feature, a short film, a commercial laid out like the AICP bid form, a
  music video, or blank.
- A rate library: keep crew rates (union and non-union) and vendor prices once, use them in any budget, and
  save a line's rate to it.
- The top sheet and the detail as a PDF (or printed), with your company's name, logo and contact; an Excel
  export with the top sheet, the detail, the globals and the fringes.
- Import budget lines from a CSV file; import and export JSON so other tools can send in rates, a schedule
  and crew, or take a budget out for invoicing.
- Light and dark, an accent color of your choice, undo and redo for everything, and it all saves itself.
